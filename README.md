# 🚀 Customer Segmentation using RFM

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes customer behavior using the **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** model to segment customers and generate actionable insights.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful customer segments that support better business decisions.
---



---

## 💡 What is RFM?
RFM is a customer segmentation technique based on:

- **Recency** → How recently a customer made a purchase  
- **Frequency** → How often a customer purchases  
- **Monetary** → How much a customer spends  

This approach helps identify key customer groups such as:
- Champions  
- Loyal Customers  
- Promising  
- At Risk  
- Lost

## 💡 Key Insights
- Promising segment represents the biggest growth opportunity  
- Loyal and Champions drive the majority of revenue  
- At Risk customers require immediate retention strategies  
- Fashion category is the top revenue contributor  
- Majority of customers are Female (~43 years old)  
- UPI is the most used payment method
